## ⚠️ ANCESTRY CONFLATION WARNING — THREE EDMUNDS
• **Severity Level:** `CRITICAL / HIGH HAZARD`  
• **Affected Person Nodes:** *Edmund Browder I (c.1651–1739), Edmund W. Browder (c.1699–1771), Edmund Browder Sr. (c.1716–1759)*  
• **Detailed Description:** Commercial genealogy databases (Ancestry.com, MyHeritage, Geni, WikiTree user trees) frequently conflate three distinct generations of Edmund Browder into a single person.  
• **Empirical Proof & Evidence:** Primary court records prove: (1) Edmund Browder I died ~1738/39 in Prince George Co. VA; (2) Edmund W. Browder owned Browder's Island and died impoverished in 1771 (1759 Act of Assembly); (3) Edmund Browder Sr. married Elizabeth Jones and died 1759 (Bristol Parish Registers).  
• **Required Examination Action:** `Never merge these three individuals. Treat user trees combining them as corrupted.`

## ⚠️ FALSE IRISH COAT OF ARMS & BRODERICK CONFLATION
• **Severity Level:** `HIGH HAZARD`  
• **Affected Person Nodes:** *O'Bruadair / Broderick / Broderbund*  
• **Detailed Description:** Claims online assert that Browder is an anglicized form of Irish O'Bruadair or Broderick and entitled to the Broderick Coat of Arms.  
• **Empirical Proof & Evidence:** Dr. Benjamin Hudson (Medievalist) and J.M. Browder prove Browder is an Old English occupational contraction of 'browderer' (embroiderer). Broderick derives from Welsh 'ap Rhydderch'. The Broderick Coat of Arms has ZERO historical connection to Browder.  
• **Required Examination Action:** `Reject all Irish O'Bruadair heraldry and Broderick coat of arms attachments.`
