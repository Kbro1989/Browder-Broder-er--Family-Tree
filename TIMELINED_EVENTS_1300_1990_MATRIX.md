# ⏳ TIMELINED EVENTS MATRIX (1300 – 1990)

Comprehensive chronological cross-reference matrix of all primary evidence, parish registers, land grants, wills, battles, and person milestones across **690 years (1300–1990)**.

## 📅 14th Century (1300-1399)

### 📍 **1327** — Flemish Weavers Invited to England & John Browderer Earliest Record
• **Location:** `London & St. Hilary Parish, Cornwall`  
• **Persons Involved:** *John Browderer, Roger Trewynnarde, Sir Walter Sancre, King Edward III*  
• **Primary Source:** `St. Hilary Parish Website, J.M. Browder Paper, Llewellyn (1936)`  
• **Historical Details:** King Edward III encourages Flemish weavers to settle in England. John Browderer (King's Sergeant at Arms) involved in St. Hilary Parish incident; beheaded priest Sir Walter Sancre.  

### 📍 **1348** — The Black Death Sweeps London Weavers
• **Location:** `London, England`  
• **Persons Involved:** *Flemish Weavers Community*  
• **Primary Source:** `Worshipful Company of Weavers History`  
• **Historical Details:** Black Plague devastating London population. Weavers quarter heavily affected; guild structures survive under Crown protection.  

### 📍 **1394** — John Browderer Documented as Citizen of London
• **Location:** `London, England`  
• **Persons Involved:** *John Browderer*  
• **Primary Source:** `Entries in Papal Registers (Papal Letters Vol. IV p. 488; Lateran Regesta Vol. XXXIV)`  
• **Historical Details:** Notation on 4 Kal. May (May 4, 1394) recording 'John Browderer, citizen of London'. Earliest recorded usage of Browderer as a formal surname.

• **Historical Details:** Will executed April 22, 1459 bequeaths 'To William Browderer 53s, 4d'. Proves high-status association with nobility.  

### 📍 **1466** — Richard Browderer at Coldharbour, Cornwall

### 📍 **1497** — Pact Between Native & Alien Weavers Guilds
• **Location:** `London, England`  
• **Persons Involved:** *Flemish & Native Weavers Guilds*  
• **Primary Source:** `Worshipful Company of Weavers History`

### 📍 **1642** — George Browder Documented in Dublin Chancery Court Suit
• **Location:** `Dublin, Ireland`

• **Primary Source:** `Dublin Chancery Court Records, J.M. Browder Blog (Feb 2023)`  
• **Historical Details:** Lawsuit in Dublin Chancery Court naming George Browder (b. c. 1624). Proves Browder family presence in Ireland prior to 1650.  

### 📍 **1664** — Thomas Browder Assessed in Tipperary Hearth Money Roll
• **Location:** `Ballynoran, Shunraghan Parish, Baronies of Iffa & Offa, Co. Tipperary, Ireland`

• **Historical Details:** Thomas Browder (b. c. 1624) assessed for 1 hearth in Ballynoran, Tipperary. Confirms Browder residency in Ireland.  

### 📍 **1674** — John Broadar [Browder] Headright Land Grant on Elizabeth River, VA
• **Location:** `Lower Norfolk County, Virginia`  
• **Persons Involved:** *John Broadar, William Havercomb, James Jopling, John Porter Sr., Isaac Hocker*  
• **Primary Source:** `Virginia Land Patents Book 6, p. 526 (Sept 23, 1674)`  
• **Historical Details:** William Havercomb granted 950 acres for transporting 18 persons, including John Broadar [Browder]. Land situated on South side of Eastern Branch of Elizabeth River.  

### 📍 **1682** — Edmund Browder I Documented in Surry County VA Court Suit
• **Location:** `Surry County, Virginia`  
• **Persons Involved:** *Edmund Browder I, Sheriff Robert Bolling*  
• **Primary Source:** `Surry County Orders, Deeds, Wills (1682, 1695)`  
• **Historical Details:** Edmund Browder I (b. c. 1651) involved in court suit regarding serge suits and French fall shoes. Merchant merchant dealing in imported English cloth.

### 📍 **1739** — Will of Edmund Browder I Probated in Prince George County
• **Location:** `Prince George County, Virginia`  
• **Persons Involved:** *Edmund Browder I, Elizabeth Parsons, John Browder Sr., Edmund W. Browder, George Andrew Browder*  
• **Primary Source:** `Prince George County Will Book (1738/39)`  
• **Historical Details:** Edmund Browder I dies at ~88 years of age. Will probated in Prince George County, establishing the Tidewater Virginia patriarch line.  

### 📍 **1759** — Virginia Act of Assembly Concerning Browder's Island Appomattox Bridge
• **Location:** `Browder's Island, Appomattox River, Chesterfield County, VA`

• **Primary Source:** `Hening's Statutes at Large / 1759 Act of Assembly`  
• **Historical Details:** Act of Assembly passed regarding bridge across Appomattox River at Browder's Island (site of old Fort Henry). Edmund W. Browder later dies impoverished in 1771.  

### 📍 **1776** — Browder Revolutionary War Mobilization
• **Location:** `Dinwiddie & Prince George Counties, Virginia`  
• **Persons Involved:** *Captain Isham Browder, Lt. Joseph Browder, Jesse Browder, Charles Browder*  
• **Primary Source:** `Virginia Revolutionary War Pension Files & Sworn Depositions`  
• **Historical Details:** Capt. Isham Browder, Lt. Joseph Browder, Jesse Browder, and Charles Browder enlist in Continental Army / Virginia Militia.  

### 📍 **1780** — Sworn Testimony of Peter Eppes Regarding Fallen Sons Jesse & Charles
• **Location:** `Dinwiddie County, Virginia`  
• **Persons Involved:** *Peter Eppes, Jesse Browder, Charles Browder, William Browder Sr.*  
• **Primary Source:** `Sworn Deposition of Peter Eppes, J.M. Browder Research`  
• **Historical Details:** Peter Eppes provides sworn court testimony confirming that Jesse and Charles Browder (sons of William Sr. & Margaret) died in Revolutionary War service.
